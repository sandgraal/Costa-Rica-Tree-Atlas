<AccordionItem title="🔬 Taxonomic Notes">

The genus _Calophyllum_ contains about 190 species worldwide, mostly in tropical Asia. The name means "beautiful leaf" in Greek. The family Calophyllaceae was recently separated from Clusiaceae.

**Related Species:**

- _Calophyllum inophyllum_ — Indo-Pacific species, coastal
- _Calophyllum longifolium_ — Costa Rican mountains

**Notable Compound**: Cedro María produces "tamanu oil" precursors, related to the famous oil from _C. inophyllum_.
