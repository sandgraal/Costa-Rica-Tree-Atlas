# Gallinazo

<Callout type="success" title="The Speed Demon">
  **Gallinazo** (_Schizolobium parahyba_) is nature's skyscraper builder,
  shooting up to 30 meters in just 5-7 years. When this legume giant blooms, its
  crown transforms into a spectacular golden dome visible from kilometers away,
  earning it the nickname "tower of gold."

<AccordionItem title="🔬 Taxonomic Notes">

The genus _Schizolobium_ contains only 2 species, both from the neotropics. The name comes from Greek "schizo" (split) and "lobium" (pod), referring to the fruit.

**Related Species:**

- _Schizolobium amazonicum_ — Similar Amazonian species

**Classification Note**: Some authorities consider _S. parahyba_ and _S. amazonicum_ as varieties of the same species, but they are generally treated as distinct.
