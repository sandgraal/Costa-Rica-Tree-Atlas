# Fruta Dorada

<Callout type="success" title="The Wildlife Feast Tree">
  The **Fruta Dorada** (_Virola koschnyi_), or **Golden Fruit tree**, is a
  majestic rainforest canopy tree whose distinctive bright red fruits split open
  to reveal golden-yellow arils that are irresistible to toucans, monkeys, and
  dozens of other wildlife species. This member of the nutmeg family is a
  keystone species for rainforest food webs.
</Callout>

<AccordionItem title="🔬 Taxonomic Notes">

The genus _Virola_ contains about 45 species distributed through the Neotropics, all in the nutmeg family (Myristicaceae). The species was named after botanist Bernhard Koschny who collected specimens in Costa Rica.

**Family Traits**: Like the commercial nutmeg (_Myristica fragrans_), Virola fruits have a distinctive structure—a single seed covered by a brightly colored, fleshy aril within a splitting capsule.

**Red Exudate**: When the bark is cut, _Virola_ species produce a distinctive reddish sap that oxidizes dark—hence the local name "Sangre" (blood).

</AccordionItem>
</Accordion>
